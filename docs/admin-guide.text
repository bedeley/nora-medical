## Overview
- What it is: A back-office portal for operations, accounting, HR, and settings.
- How to use: Start at /admin/dashboard, then use the top nav or Admin menu.
- Related: Most pages are role-gated; STAFF/ACCOUNTANT see limited options.

## Access & Security
- Pages: /admin/mfa, /unauthorized
- What it is: Admin 2FA setup and access control feedback.
- How to use: Enable 2FA on first admin login; use TOTP codes to access admin routes.
- Related: Admin sessions expire and are enforced in middleware.
- Role gates: Admin-only actions include purchase creation/receiving, returns/refunds, supplier payments, and journal approvals.
- Users & roles: Manage staff roles in /admin/users (role changes require NEXT_PUBLIC_ADMIN_ROLE_MANAGEMENT_ENABLED=1). Use “Invite employee” to send a 24-hour invite link with verification code via email ? SMS ? WhatsApp fallback.

## Users & Roles
- Page: /admin/users
- What it is: The staff identity and access workspace. Use it to invite staff user accounts, assign roles, repair HR profile links, monitor invite status, and manage session/access controls without going through the customer list.
- When to use it:
  - When a new employee already exists in HR and now needs portal access.
  - When a role changes between Staff, Dispatcher, Accountant, and Admin.
  - When an older bootstrapped account is missing an HR profile link.
  - When a staff account needs deactivation, reactivation, or forced logout.
- Data rule:
  - This page manages user identity and access state.
  - HR remains the source of truth for the full employee profile.
  - `Start HR onboarding` is the employee-create handoff when the employee record does not exist yet.
  - `Create HR profile` only creates or links the HR employee record; it does not complete the full staff profile details.

## Dashboard & Recent Activity
- Pages: /admin/dashboard, /admin/recent
- What it is: The redesigned management dashboard. It separates ledger-backed financial reporting from operational workflow context so the team can review sales, cash, exceptions, and trend charts from one page.
- Default entry: `/admin/dashboard?groupBy=day` is the normal daily view. `day` is the baseline grouping; switch to `week`, `month`, or `year` only when you need a wider trend view.
- Data-source rule:
  - Financial KPIs use posted GL values where available.
  - `Financial reporting` is the main ledger-backed section for revenue, discounts, refunds, tax, expenses, profit, and cash.
  - `Operational context` keeps collections, outstanding balances, orders, and fulfillment in the live operational layer.
  - `Cash` cards are payment-date based.
  - `Ledger Alignment` compares operational and posted-ledger numbers for the same filters.

Page walkthrough - top to bottom

1) Header actions
   - Title: `Dashboard`.
   - Subtitle explains that the page tracks revenue, expenses, and cash flow at a glance.
   - Helper text explains that financial reporting is ledger-backed while collections, outstanding balances, and fulfillment remain operational.
   - Action buttons:
     - `Add Expense`
     - `Refresh`
     - `Raw Data`
     - `CSV`
     - `PDF`
     - `Copy Link`
   - Use `CSV` and `PDF` to export the current filtered view, not a separate unfiltered report.

2) Health banner and anomaly chips
   - If health issues exist, an amber banner appears with counts such as stock, balance, payment, or legacy issues and a `Review health check` link.
   - Small anomaly chips can appear below the banner, for example:
     - high outstanding ratio
     - negative net cash
     - ledger alignment review
   - Treat these as triage prompts, not final diagnosis.

3) Filter row
   - `Period` shows the active start/end range and current grouping.
   - `Refreshed` shows the most recent successful data refresh time.
   - `Comparison On/Off` controls previous-period delta labels on key cards.
   - `Reset` clears start, end, customer, category, and returns grouping to `day`.
   - Date picker quick ranges include `Today`, `Last 7 days`, `This month`, `Last 30 days`, `This quarter`, `Last quarter`, `Last 12 months`, and `This year`.
   - `This quarter`, `Last quarter`, and `Last 12 months` switch grouping to `month` automatically.
   - Customer and category filters sync to the URL so the current view can be shared with `Copy Link`.

4) Snapshot + accounting status row
   - `ProfitSummary` appears first as the high-level KPI strip.
   - Three compact status cards follow:
     - `Open period`
     - `Latest VAT filing`
     - `Integrity status`
   - Use these cards to confirm whether accounting period control and integrity checks are healthy before trusting the trend story.

5) Alerts, expense mix, and quick links
   - Operational widgets follow:
     - inventory alerts
     - margin risk
     - monitoring summary
   - `Expense breakdown` shows payroll first, then other categories ranked by share of total expense.
   - Quick links:
     - `Review Outstanding`
     - `Review Pending MoMo`
     - `Open Integrity`
     - `Open Health`
   - `Metric definitions` explains the financial-versus-operational split. Use it when there is confusion over ledger-backed metrics versus workflow metrics.

6) `Ledger Alignment`
   - This card compares operational and posted-ledger values for the same filters.
   - Each row shows:
     - operational value
     - ledger value
     - delta
     - status badge
   - Status meaning:
     - `Aligned`
     - `Within GH₵1`
     - `Review`
   - Use this card to monitor whether the two reporting bases are converging cleanly enough to retire the operational financial view later.

7) `Financial reporting`, `Cash`, payroll, and `Operational context`
   - `Financial reporting` contains the ledger-backed revenue and profitability cards:
     - `Gross sales (pre-tax)`
     - `Tax collected`
     - `Discounts`
     - `Net sales`
     - `Refunds`
   - `Cash (payment-date basis)` contains:
     - `Cash in`
     - `Cash out`
     - `Net cash`
   - `Payroll expense` is shown as its own follow-on detail card so payroll can be read as part of the financial story.
   - `Operational context` separates workflow and AR interpretation from financial reporting.
   - Use these sections intentionally:
     - financial cards for financial reporting
     - operational cards for collections, outstanding, and fulfillment explanation

8) `Operational context` details
   - `Period sales reconciliation` explains the current-period relationship:
     - `Billed order total (incl. tax) = Collected on period sales + Outstanding on period sales`
   - The mobile `Show operational context` button expands the operational cards on small screens.
   - Operational cards include:
     - `Outstanding (period orders)`
     - orders and AOV
     - fulfillment split
   - Use this section when collections, open balances, or delivery-state workflow need explanation.

9) Ranked lists
   - `Top products (by quantity)` lists the strongest-selling SKUs for the current date range.
   - `Top customers` ranks accounts by order value and shows available credit when relevant.
   - If there is no data for the selected range, these panels may be absent.

10) Charts
   - `Sales Trend (Gross vs Net)`
   - `Expense Trend`
   - `Cash Flow`
   - `Orders & AOV`
   - `Margin Trend`
   - When using `day`, expect the densest operational view. Use `month` when leadership wants cleaner trend direction and payroll month-over-month context.

11) Raw Data dialog
   - `Raw Data` opens a modal listing the backing records used by the dashboard pull.
   - If no records are available, the dialog shows `No records found` with reload actions instead of a table.

How to use the page in practice
- Start on `/admin/dashboard?groupBy=day`.
- Check the health banner and anomaly chips first.
- Confirm the period and grouping before reading any KPI.
- Read `Ledger Alignment` before trusting a surprising number.
- Read `Financial reporting` and `Cash` separately.
- Use `Operational context` only for workflow interpretation, not as the final financial source of truth.
- Use quick links to drill into balances, pending MoMo, integrity, or health when a card looks off.
- Switch to `month` for quarter or rolling-12-month management review.
- Export only after the filters and grouping match the story you want to share.

## Profit & Loss
- Pages: /admin/profit-loss, /admin/profit-loss/products
- What it is: The main management P&L page. Use it to review revenue, refunds, discounts, COGS, expenses, margins, and trend behavior with either Operational View or Posted Ledger View.
- Do not confuse it with: `/admin/accounting/reports/pl`, which is the formal accounting statement page. `/admin/profit-loss` is the operational-management report with source switching, KPI cards, alignment checks, operational context, and product drill-down.
- Source rule:
  - `Operational View` uses live order, return, payment, and expense activity.
  - `Posted Ledger View` uses posted journal entries for financial reporting.
  - The page can override the default source from Accounting Settings without changing the global setting.
  - The `Ledger Alignment` block compares both sources for the same filters and flags any drift above GH₵1.

Top-to-bottom walkthrough

1) Header and page actions
   - Title: `Profit & Loss`.
   - Subtitle explains that the page reviews revenue, costs, and margins over time.
   - The helper text explains the difference between `Operational View` and `Posted Ledger View`.
   - Header actions:
     - `View Product Performance`
     - `Export CSV` and `Export PDF` when the page is in Operational View
     - `Open Accounting Reports` when the page is in Posted Ledger View
   - Read the `Current source` line before interpreting the numbers.

2) `Report View`
   - This card controls the reporting basis for the current page.
   - Buttons:
     - `Operational View`
     - `Posted Ledger View`
     - `Use Default`
   - `Use Default` clears the page override and returns to the Accounting Settings default.
   - The note below the buttons explains:
     - the current default source from settings
     - whether the page is currently using an override
     - whether the page is reading posted journals or operational activity

3) `Filters`
   - Controls:
     - `Start`
     - `End`
     - `Group by`
     - `Customer`
     - `Expense Category`
     - `Exclude zero periods`
   - The page shows period count plus a `Coverage` note explaining exactly what data is included for the active source.
   - The URL stores filter state and source override so the view can be reopened or shared exactly.

4) `Financial KPIs`
   - This is the main KPI strip for the page.
   - Cards include:
     - `Revenue`
     - `Discounts`
     - `Net Sales (after discounts, before refunds)`
     - `Refunds`
     - `Net Revenue`
     - `Gross Profit`
     - `COGS`
     - `Expenses`
     - `Net Profit`
     - `Tax Collected`
     - `Gross Margin`
     - `Operating Margin`
   - Important calculation rule:
     - Gross profit and gross margin are based on net revenue, not gross revenue.
   - In Posted Ledger View, the page may show some detail as unavailable if the ledger route does not expose that metric separately for the selected period.
   - The text below the cards states whether the KPIs are coming from posted journals or operational activity.
   - `Trend window` changes the sparkline history shown inside the KPI cards.

5) `Ledger Alignment`
   - This card compares Operational and Posted Ledger values for the same filters.
   - Each row shows:
     - Operational value
     - Ledger value
     - Delta
     - status badge
   - Status meaning:
     - `Aligned` means the numbers match
     - `Within GH₵1` means the difference is small and inside tolerance
     - `Review` means the difference is material and should be investigated
   - Use this block before retiring Operational View. It is the page-level reconciliation check.

6) `Expense Breakdown`
   - Shows expense categories and amounts for the current filters.
   - Use this section to see which expense categories are driving the selected period.
   - If there are no expenses in scope, the page shows a clear empty state instead of blank rows.

7) `Operational Context`
   - This section separates workflow metrics from financial reporting.
   - In `Operational View`, it shows:
     - `Orders`
     - `Avg Order Value`
     - `Delivered`
     - `Partial`
     - `Returned`
     - `Pending`
   - In `Posted Ledger View`, the page explains that workflow metrics stay operational and should be reviewed here only after switching source or by using the dashboard.

8) `Trend Overview`
   - This is the SVG trend chart area.
   - `Download chart` exports the visible chart as an SVG.
   - Series legend appears above the chart and the x-axis uses formatted period labels, not raw date keys.
   - Use this section for visual trend direction across the selected grouping.

9) Trend table / mobile cards
   - Desktop shows the full trend table with formatted period labels.
   - Mobile shows stacked period cards instead of the full table.
   - The table includes a totals row so the page-level period totals can be checked directly in the table.
   - Pagination controls and `Rows per page` apply to the trend table.

10) Product drill-down
   - `View Product Performance` opens `/admin/profit-loss/products`.
   - Use it when the page-level P&L looks unusual and you need to isolate product-level contribution.
   - Product P&L now deducts return effects instead of overstating revenue and quantity.

How to use the page in practice
- Start with the correct source.
- Confirm the date range, grouping, and customer/category filters.
- Read the `Financial KPIs` first, then check `Ledger Alignment` if the source story looks questionable.
- Use `Expense Breakdown` to understand which cost categories are driving the result.
- Use `Operational Context` only for workflow interpretation, not as the final financial source of truth.
- Use `Trend Overview` and the trend table to decide whether the issue is isolated to one period or part of a broader pattern.
- Use `View Product Performance` when the page-level story needs product-level explanation.

## Product Profit & Loss
- Page: /admin/profit-loss/products
- What it is: The product-level drill-down from the main management P&L. Use it to rank products by net quantity, revenue, margin, and profit after return deductions.
- How to use it: Start here when the page-level P&L looks unusual and you need to isolate which SKUs are driving profit, loss, or margin compression.
- Data rule:
  - Product totals are built from order items in the selected period.
  - Returned quantity and refunded revenue are deducted from product totals.
  - COGS is reversed only for returns that were actually restocked.
  - `Summary` reflects the full filtered result set for the period, not just the current page of rows.

Top-to-bottom walkthrough

1) Header and quick actions
   - Breadcrumb: `Profit & Loss / Products`.
   - Header title: `Product Performance (P&L)`.
   - Subtitle explains that the page ranks products by profit, margin, and revenue.
   - The blue range line shows the active date window and whether `Loss-makers only` is enabled.
   - Header actions:
     - `Back`
     - `This Week`
     - `Today`
     - `Clear Filters`
     - `Export CSV`
     - `Export PDF`
   - `Back` returns to `/admin/profit-loss`.
   - `Alt + Left Arrow` is the keyboard shortcut back to the main P&L page.

2) `Filters`
   - The first row includes:
     - `Columns`
     - `Top 10`
     - `Top 25`
     - `Top 50`
     - `Loss-makers only`
   - `Columns` controls whether the table shows:
     - `Weighted Cost (per item)`
     - `Weighted Sold Price (per item)`
     - `Total Weighted Cost`
   - The main filter grid includes:
     - `Search products`
     - `Range`
     - `Start`
     - `End`
     - `Sort`
     - `Rows`
   - `Range` supports:
     - `Today`
     - `This Week`
     - `This Month`
     - `Last 12 months`
     - `All time`
     - `Custom`
   - `Start` and `End` are active only when `Range` is `Custom`.
   - `Sort` chooses the ranking metric:
     - `Profit`
     - `Revenue`
     - `Net Quantity`
     - `Margin %`
   - The adjacent button flips the list between `Best -> Worst` and `Worst -> Best`.
   - `Rows` controls the visible page size.
   - Filter state is stored in the URL so the view can be reopened or shared.
   - The page also remembers the last custom date range locally until filters are cleared.

3) `Summary`
   - This card shows full-period totals for all matching products, not just the visible rows.
   - KPIs:
     - `All products in period`
     - `Net qty sold`
     - `Total revenue`
     - `Total profit`
   - The subtitle shows the active range and any active search term.
   - If `Loss-makers only` is on, the note clarifies that the loss filter applies only to the table, not to the period summary.

4) `Performance Details`
   - Desktop uses a table; mobile uses stacked cards.
   - Core row fields:
     - `Rank`
     - `Product`
     - `View`
     - `Net Qty`
     - `Revenue`
     - `Margin %`
     - `Profit / Loss`
   - Optional columns appear when enabled from `Columns`:
     - `Weighted Cost`
     - `Weighted Sold Price`
     - `Total Weighted Cost`
   - `View` opens the inventory page filtered to that product.
   - Profit values show positive/negative emphasis to make loss-makers easier to spot quickly.
   - The row counter above the table shows `Showing X of Y products`.

5) Empty, loading, and error states
   - Loading state shows a simple `Loading...` message in the table or mobile card area.
   - Error state shows `Failed to load product P&L`.
   - Empty state shows `No data for the current filters.` plus:
     - `Reset filters`
     - `View products`
   - `Reset filters` now clears persisted custom-range memory as well as current URL filters.

6) Pagination
   - Pagination appears when more than one page of products exists.
   - Previous, next, first, last, and nearby page links are shown.
   - If a bookmarked URL requests an out-of-range page, the API clamps to the last valid page and the page state normalizes to that real page instead of showing a false empty state.

How to use the page well
- Start with `Profit` sort and `Best -> Worst` for the default margin story.
- Switch to `Loss-makers only` when you are triaging negative contribution products.
- Use `Revenue` sort when the concern is sales concentration rather than profitability.
- Keep `Summary` and `Performance Details` separate in your reading: the summary is full-period, while the table is paginated.
- Use `Columns` to hide cost detail when you only need ranking and expose it when margin compression needs explanation.
- Use the inventory `View` link on an unusual row to inspect stock position and movement context for that product.

## Payments — MoMo Settlement & Reconciliation
- Page: /admin/payments/momo
- What it is: Ghana-specific MoMo monitoring for provider requests, manual records, and reconciliation outcomes.
- How to use:
  - Use Status + Provider filters to isolate pending vs settled.
  - `Source` helps interpretation:
    - `Provider`: initiated from app and waiting/confirmed from provider callbacks.
    - `Manual`: recorded external payment; treated as settled from operations side.
  - `Ledger: Posted/Unposted` shows whether the journal entry exists for the payment.
  - The `Actions` column only appears when at least one visible row is actionable.
    - If all rows are view-only, the column is hidden.
  - For long-running pending provider requests:
    1) Open row and click `Cancel pending`.
    2) Collect alternate payment if needed (cash/transfer/manual MoMo).
    3) If provider later returns success, row becomes `late_success_after_cancel`.
    4) Click `Resolve to Credit` to move amount to customer store-credit liability.
- When to act:
  - Pending for long periods: cancel and use alternate payment flow (avoid duplicate collection).

- Note:
  - This page is operational control + monitoring.
  - Detailed ledger behavior for these events is documented under Accounting ? Posting Rules and Accounting ? Journal.
- Test-mode simulation (technical, not daily operations):
  - `Simulate Late Success` is hidden by default.
  - It appears only when test tools are enabled in non-production.
  - Use it only for QA/UAT to verify late-success-after-cancel handling.

  2) Add a Cron Job targeting `https://your-domain.com/api/admin/inventory-planning/recompute`.

  4) Schedule daily or weekly; the endpoint respects the toggle and last run time.

## Accounting Portal Overview
- Page: /admin/accounting
- What it is: Central hub for the accounting module. Provides live financial position, alert banners, recent journal activity, and grouped navigation to every accounting sub-module.

Page layout — top to bottom:

  1. Header
     - Title: "Accounting" with a subtitle.
     - "+ New Journal Entry" button (top-right) — opens /admin/accounting/journal?new=1 directly.

  2. Alert banners (shown conditionally, stacked)
     a) Integrity banner (amber):
        - Appears when any integrity issue is detected: draft journal entries, AR ledger mismatch, inventory ledger mismatch, or negative stock items.
        - Lists each issue by name and count inline. "Review" link → /admin/accounting/integrity.
        - Disappears automatically once all issues are cleared.
     b) Period close reminder banner (amber):
        - Appears when the nearest OPEN fiscal period ends within the configured reminder window (default 7 days, set in Accounting Settings).
        - Shows period name, days remaining, and close date.
        - Pre-close checklist (2 items, fetched from the period checklist API):
            ✓ No draft journal entries — green checkmark when count = 0; otherwise shows count with link to Journal.
            ✓ All reconciliations closed — green checkmark when count = 0; otherwise shows count with link to Reconciliations.
        - Progress shown as "X of 2 complete". Clicking a failing item navigates directly to the blocking page.

  3. Financial Position (6 KPI cards, live data)
     Each card has a left-border color: green = healthy/zero-balance, amber = needs attention, slate = neutral.
     - Cash on Hand     — account 1000 (physical cash). Links to Cash Reconciliation.
     - Bank Balance     — sum across all bank accounts (account 1010). Links to Bank Accounts.
     - Accounts Receivable — total outstanding customer balances. Links to AR Aging (?type=ar).
                            4th line: amber "GHS X overdue 61+ days" when any AR aged 61+; green "No overdue balances" when all current.
     - Accounts Payable    — total owed to suppliers. Links to AP Aging (?type=ap).
                            4th line: amber "GHS X overdue 61+ days" or green "All payables current".
     - Inventory Value  — ledger inventory at weighted average cost. Links to Inventory Valuation.
     - VAT Payable      — output VAT less input VAT. Links to VAT Filings.
     All six cards are equal height regardless of which have overdue notes.
     "Full reports →" link (top-right of section) → /admin/accounting/reports.

  4. Recent Activity feed
     - Shows the last 5 journal entries sorted by date descending.
     - Each row: date · memo (or source type) · POSTED/DRAFT/VOID status badge · entry amount.
     - POSTED = green badge, DRAFT = amber badge, VOID = muted badge.
     - Click any row → /admin/accounting/journal.
     - "View all →" link at top-right of section.
     - Section is hidden entirely when no journal entries exist.

  5. Module groups (navigation tiles, each is a full-card link)
     Grouped into 5 sections:

     Transactions & Ledger (4 tiles):
       Journal Entries, Chart of Accounts, Posting Rules, Tax Codes.

     Reconciliation & Banking (4 tiles):
       Bank Reconciliation — shows amber "N pending" badge when draft or in-progress reconciliations exist.
       Cash Reconciliation, Reconcile Totals, Bank Accounts.

     Receivables, Payables & Inventory (3 tiles):
       AR Aging, AP Aging, Inventory Valuation.

     Reports & Compliance (3 tiles):
       Financial Reports, Profit & Loss, VAT Filings.

     Administration (5 tiles/actions):
       Fiscal Periods, Data Integrity, Owner Equity (dialog), Opening Retained Earnings (dialog), Settings.

  6. Owner Equity dialog (Administration section)
     - "Record contribution" opens an in-page dialog. Does not navigate away.
     - Use this for new money the owner is putting into the business after go-live.
     - Fields: Deposit to (Cash / Bank account selector), Amount, Notes (optional).
     - If Bank is selected: a second dropdown lists bank accounts by name + masked number.
     - On save: posts a balanced journal entry — Debit Cash (1000) or Bank (1010), Credit Owner Equity (3000).
     - Validation: amount must be a positive number; bank account required when source = Bank.

  7. Opening Retained Earnings dialog (Administration section)
     - "Set opening balance" opens an in-page dialog. After first use, the action changes to "View setup".
     - Use this once at go-live when the business already has retained earnings from before this app was adopted.
     - Function: reclassifies part of Opening Balance Equity into Retained Earnings so the balance sheet reflects pre-app accumulated profit correctly.
     - Entry posted: Debit Opening Balance Equity (3900), Credit Retained Earnings (3100).
     - Fields before setup: Effective date, Retained earnings amount, Notes (optional).
     - Validation and controls:
         amount must be a positive number;
         entry date cannot fall in a closed fiscal period;
         setup is locked after first successful save.
     - After setup:
         dialog shows configured amount, effective date, notes, and journal entry id;
         "Open journal entry" jumps directly to the posted journal;
         corrections should be handled with a controlled journal adjustment, not by rerunning the setup.

Day-start workflow:
  1) Check Integrity banner — resolve any listed issues before starting operations.
  2) Check Period close banner — complete checklist items if period is ending soon.
  3) Review Financial Position — verify Cash on Hand and Bank Balance match your expectation.
  4) Review Recent Activity — spot any unexpected DRAFT entries that need posting or approval.

Test strips:
  - Integrity banner: create a draft journal entry → banner should appear with count. Post the entry → banner clears.
  - Period close checklist: ensure a fiscal period ends within the reminder window → banner appears. Verify checklist reflects accurate draft-entry and open-reconciliation counts.
  - Financial Position — AR overdue: create a customer order with a balance dated 62+ days ago → AR card shows amber "GHS X overdue 61+ days" line.
  - Financial Position — card heights: all 6 KPI cards render at equal height regardless of overdue note content.
  - Recent Activity: post a new journal entry → it appears as the first row in the feed with a POSTED badge and correct amount.
  - Bank Reconciliation badge: create a draft reconciliation → "N pending" badge appears on the Bank Reconciliation tile.
  - Owner Equity: open dialog, enter a valid amount with source = Bank but no bank selected → error toast. Select a bank, submit → success toast and dialog closes; a new journal entry should appear in Recent Activity.
  - Opening Retained Earnings: open dialog, leave amount blank or enter 0 → validation should block save. Enter a valid amount and date in an open period, submit → success toast, dialog closes, and the action changes from "Set opening balance" to "View setup".
  - Opening Retained Earnings lock: reopen the dialog after successful setup → configured amount/date/journal id are shown and the one-time setup cannot be submitted again from the page.
  - Fiscal period baseline: on a fresh install with no periods, the system auto-creates one OPEN period for the current calendar year on first load of any accounting page.

## Health Check & Operations
- Pages: /admin/health, /admin/health/incidents
- What it is: The accounting and operations exception board. Use it to confirm that ledger totals, posting completeness, stock movements, payment allocations, and POD compliance are aligned before you trust dashboard or report totals.
- When to use it:
  - Start of day, before finance review or dispatch follow-up.
  - After bulk imports, reconciliation work, backfills, or accounting fixes.
  - Before period close, audit export, or management reporting.
- Data-source rule:
  - /admin/health is the summary-and-triage page. It shows counts, mismatch amounts, action links, and repair tools.
  - /admin/accounting/integrity is the drilldown page for accounting-specific diagnosis.
  - /admin/health/incidents is the response log and handoff trail for tracked health incidents.

Page walkthrough - top to bottom

1) Header
   - Title: `Health Check`.
   - Subtitle links to `/admin/accounting/integrity` for deeper accounting-only review.

2) Health Operations panel
   - This is the action layer at the top of the page.
   - It includes the triage queue, runbook-by-signal view, export actions, alert controls, and incident shortcuts.
   - Treat this panel as the place to decide what to work first, not as the final evidence table.

3) Triage queue
   - Each row is a direct operational exception with an `Open queue` link.
   - `Ledger mismatches` shows a count of failing ledger checks, not a transaction count.
   - The ledger check count is built from these checks:
     - AR ledger vs customer balances
     - Inventory ledger vs stock valuation
     - AP ledger vs outstanding purchases
     - Trial balance out of balance
   - Queue routing:
     - `Ledger mismatches` -> `#ledger-integrity`
     - `Missing postings` -> `#ledger-readiness`
     - `Stock mismatches` -> `#stock-movement-mismatches`
     - `Order balance mismatches` -> `#order-balance-mismatches`
     - `Payment mismatches` -> `#payment-mismatches`
     - `Legacy auto-apply` -> `#legacy-auto-apply`
     - `POD compliance alert` -> `#pod-compliance`

4) Runbook by signal
   - These cards group related issues by theme and each card has an `Open signal view` link.
   - `Posting + ledger` shows combined open issue count across failing ledger checks plus missing posting buckets.
   - Signal routing:
     - `Posting + ledger` -> `#ledger-integrity`
     - `Stock integrity` -> `#stock-movement-mismatches`
     - `Payments + balances` -> `#data-quality`
     - `POD compliance` -> `#pod-compliance`

5) Severity banners
   - Warning or error banners summarize the highest-severity active problems near the top of the page.
   - Use these as escalation signals when deciding same-day response priority.

6) Ledger integrity
   - Anchor: `#ledger-integrity`
   - This section compares ledger balances against operational truth for AR, inventory, AP, and trial-balance balance.
   - A warning state here means the GL cannot be trusted at face value until the mismatch is explained or repaired.

7) Ledger readiness
   - Anchor: `#ledger-readiness`
   - This shows posting completeness by source type, including orders, payments, expenses, purchases, supplier payments, credit payouts, and settlements.
   - Use it to confirm that real-world activity has actually reached the ledger.

8) Recent posting failures
   - Lists the most recent failed or skipped posting events from the audit trail.
   - Review this table before doing manual backfills so you know whether the issue is current or historical.

9) Data quality
   - Anchor: `#data-quality`
   - This section summarizes mismatches between denormalized order fields and underlying payment/order math.
   - `#payment-mismatches`: order `amountPaid` differs from summed linked payments.
   - `#order-balance-mismatches`: order `balance` differs from recalculated outstanding balance.
   - For AP review, rely on the page totals: the app normalizes liability signs correctly, so the AP mismatch amount on the page is the correct reference value.

10) Operational checks
   - Includes stock movement mismatches, negative stock, and legacy auto-apply checks.
   - Use these to separate pure accounting drift from inventory or allocation defects.

11) POD compliance
   - Anchor: `#pod-compliance`
   - Shows recent proof-of-delivery completion health for dispatched orders over the rolling alert window.
   - Use this before following up on settlement, dispatch, or delivery-control issues.

12) Orders and financial summary
   - This is the roll-up area that gives context on order counts, totals, and operational exposure.
   - Read it after triage so you can judge how material the open issues are.

13) Detail tables
   - `#stock-movement-mismatches`: products where current stock does not equal summed inventory movement delta.
   - `#negative-stock`: products with movement-derived stock below zero.
   - `#legacy-auto-apply`: old auto-apply payment artifacts that need review or cleanup.

14) Health incidents
   - Page: `/admin/health/incidents`
   - Use this when an issue needs ownership, notes, split/merge handling, or shift handoff.
   - This is the audit-friendly trail for incident response, not the main diagnostics page.

How to use the page well
- Start with the top panel and banners to decide severity.
- Open the linked section instead of scanning the whole page manually.
- Confirm whether the issue is a count problem, an amount problem, or a posting-completeness problem.
- Fix source data first where possible, then use backfill/repair actions only after you understand the cause.
- Recheck the section after each fix so you confirm the mismatch actually clears.

Test strip

- Click `Open queue` on `Ledger mismatches` and confirm the page scrolls to `#ledger-integrity`.
- Click `Open queue` on `Missing postings` and confirm the page scrolls to `#ledger-readiness`.
- Click `Open queue` on `Payment mismatches` and confirm the page scrolls to `#payment-mismatches`.
- Click `Open queue` on `Order balance mismatches` and confirm the page scrolls to `#order-balance-mismatches`.
- Click `Open signal view` on `Posting + ledger` and confirm the page scrolls to `#ledger-integrity`.
- Click `Open signal view` on `Payments + balances` and confirm the page scrolls to `#data-quality`.

## Accounting — Journal
- Page: /admin/accounting/journal
- What it is: The journal review and entry workbench for posted, draft, void, and archived GL entries. This page is used for search, review, posting, export, archive operations, and manual draft creation.
- Roles and permissions:
  - `ADMIN` and `ACCOUNTANT` can open and review the page.
  - Roles with `journal.post` can post drafts from the page. In the current setup that includes `ADMIN` and `ACCOUNTANT`.
  - Manual entry creation is admin-facing workflow.
  - Archive controls are admin-only.

- Top-to-bottom walkthrough:
  1) Header and open-period context:
     - Shows the page title, short description, and current open fiscal period when one exists.

  2) Export/action strip:
     - `Export CSV (filtered)`
     - `Export PDF (filtered)`
     - `Export audit pack`
     - `Export audit pack PDF`
     - `Show manual entry` / `Hide manual entry`
     - Filtered exports now use the full filtered scope, not only the visible page.

  3) Journal policy summary card:
     - Summarizes the effective runtime policy from accounting settings.
     - Shows:
       - recent default window,
       - whether manual P&L accounts are allowed,
       - archive default months,
       - archive cron dry-run/live mode,
       - large-amount anomaly threshold.

  4) Manual entry cards (shown only when `Show manual entry` is enabled):
     - `Quick manual entry`
       - Single-amount draft entry flow.
       - Requires date, reason, category, amount, debit account, and credit account.
       - Supports exception note and optional prior-period reference.
       - Prevents same-account debit/credit and zero-amount saves.
     - `Full journal entry`
       - Multi-line draft entry flow.
       - Supports account, debit, credit, optional tax code, and description per line.
       - Shows running debit/credit totals and blocks unbalanced entries.
       - Includes helpers like VAT sale template.
     - Manual entries save as drafts first and must be posted afterward.
     - When policy blocks manual P&L entry, only balance-sheet accounts are available. When policy allows it, Income and Expense accounts also appear.

  5) Review helper strips:
     - Quick tour/help strip explains shortcuts and review flow.
     - Draft queue strip shows draft count, oldest draft age/memo, and helper actions such as `Approve oldest`.

  6) Full-scope summary tiles:
     - Tiles show `Entries`, `Posted`, `Draft`, `Void`, `Debits`, and `Credits`.
     - These values reflect the full active scope across pages, not just the current page.

  7) Sticky filter bar:
     - Debounced server-side search: `Search memo/source`
     - Sort controls: `Date`, `Status`, `Amount`
     - Active-filter summary chips
     - `Advanced filters`
     - `Reset`
     - The table stays in place when filters open; the old inline push-down behavior is no longer used.

  8) Advanced filters drawer:
     - Scope controls:
       - period presets,
       - custom `From` / `To`,
       - include archive.
     - Review filters:
       - status,
       - source type,
       - batch/link ID,
       - account code/name search,
       - specific account dropdown,
       - account drill chips (`1100`, `2000`, `1200`, `1000`, `2300`),
       - debit/credit direction.
     - Exception and review toggles:
       - `Out-of-balance only`

     - Saved views:
       - save,
       - rename,
       - remove,
       - default view,

     - Work mode:
       - row density,
       - review mode.
     - Quick presets:
       - `Today posted`
       - `Draft only`
       - `Payments today`
     - Archive tools:
       - `Dry run archive`
       - `Run archive`
       - `Undo last batch` within the recovery window
       - archive run CSV export
       - archive timeline/status
       - `Open archive logs`

       - `Approve selected`

  9) Warning banner behavior:

  10) Journal table:

  11) Pagination and navigation:
      - Bottom controls show current row window, total rows, page-size selector, and direct page jump.
      - Keyboard review helpers include `J/K`, `Shift+J/K`, `Enter`, `A`, `Home/End`, and `G/Shift+G`.

## Accounting — Aging Hub
- Page: /admin/accounting/aging
- What it is: One-screen AR/AP control tower before drilling into detailed aging pages.
- How to use:
  - Set a shared `As-of date` once (or keep `Today`) to align AR and AP snapshots.
  - Review both side-by-side cards:
    - AR total + bucket split.
    - AP total + bucket split.
  - Use quick actions from each card:
    - AR -> `Open AR aging`, `Open collections queue`, `Open customers`.
    - AP -> `Open AP aging`, `Open supplier follow-up`, `Open suppliers`.

    - AR card: `1100 AR`, `1000 Cash`, `1030 MoMo Clearing`
    - AP card: `2000 AP`, `1000 Cash`, `2300 Accrued Expenses`
  - Use `Copy snapshot` to copy a compact text summary (as-of, AR, AP, net exposure) to clipboard for email/WhatsApp updates.
  - Watch `Last refreshed` to confirm current snapshot time.
- Net exposure:
  - `Net exposure = AR total - AP total`.
  - Color cue:
    - Green = positive (customers owe more than supplier obligations).
    - Amber = near zero.
    - Red = negative (supplier obligations exceed receivables).
- Expected:
  - AR/AP cards should update together when as-of date changes.
  - Drill-through links preserve the chosen as-of date on AR/AP detail pages.
  - `Open collections queue` opens Orders in outstanding-only mode sorted by highest balance.
  - `Open supplier follow-up` opens Supplier Payments with `Outstanding only` applied across all aging buckets.

## Accounting — AR Aging
- Page: /admin/accounting/aging/ar
- What it is: Customer receivables grouped by age buckets (0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+).
- How to use:
  - Filter by customer and optional as-of date.
  - Choose sort mode (`balance high->low`, `balance low->high`, `customer A-Z`).
  - Use bucket chips (`All`, `0-30`, `31-60`, `61-90`, `90+`) to focus follow-up queues.

## Operations - Returns & RMA

  - Applied to balance = value used to reduce existing outstanding balances.

- What it is:

## Accounting — Reconciliation Match Workspace

- What it is:

- Top-to-bottom walkthrough (how to use the page):

## Accounting — Cash Reconciliation
- Page: /admin/accounting/cash-reconciliations
- What it is: Three-step, two-mode cash control page for daily day-close and GL cash verification.
  Roles: ADMIN and ACCOUNTANT can save reconciliations and deposits. Only ADMIN can run auto-catchup.
- Page layout overview:
  The page is structured as three sequential steps followed by a history section.
  - Page header: title, description, and three global action buttons (Export CSV, Print Report,
    Deposit Cash to Bank). The deposit button is disabled until the selected day is reconciled in
    Operational mode.
  - Step 1: Filter bar — mode, cash account, scope, as-of date.
  - Alert banners: appear between Step 1 and the two-column workspace when conditions require attention.
  - Two-column workspace (Step 2 left, Step 3 right on large screens, stacked on mobile):
    - Left column: expected balance summary, operational source breakdown, till calculator (all
      conditional on mode and data).
    - Right column: the record cash count form.
  - Bottom: Reconciliation history — a single consolidated card with stats badges, quick-preset
    buttons, a collapsible filter bar, a sortable history table, and pagination.

- Top-to-bottom walkthrough (how to use the page):

  Step 1 — Select date and mode (filter bar):
  - Reconciliation mode:
    - `Operational (day cash-up)`: reconciles the net cash movement (cash in minus cash out) for
      the selected day. Default and most common for daily cash-up.
    - `Ledger (GL as-of)`: reconciles the cumulative GL cash balance (account 1000) as of the
      selected date end-of-day. Use for period-end or spot-check against the books.
  - Cash account: select which cash ledger account to reconcile (defaults to account 1000).
    Multiple cash accounts are supported if configured.
  - Operational scope (Operational mode only):
    - `All cash movements`: includes all cash journal lines (walk-in OTC, online orders, account
      collections, manual entries).
    - `OTC only`: filters to walk-in counter transactions only. Use when the cashier manages a
      separate till for counter sales.
  - As-of date: type a date or use the `Today` / `Yesterday` / `Custom` preset buttons. The preset
    buttons update the date field and also detect when the field is manually changed back to today
    or yesterday to keep the active preset highlighted correctly.
  - A mode description line appears below the filter bar confirming which reconciliation logic is
    active for the current mode selection.

  Alert banners (appear as needed between Step 1 and the workspace):
  - Negative GL balance (Ledger mode only, red card): shown when GL account 1000 has a negative
    cumulative balance as of the selected date. Displays the first day the balance went negative,
    the largest net cash-out day, and two action links to open the Journal filtered to the issue
    window and to the selected day. Investigate and correct postings before saving a reconciliation.
  - Unreconciled days (Operational mode, amber card): shown when one or more days with cash
    movements have no reconciliation record up to the as-of date. Shows oldest and newest missing
    day, sample dates, and two actions:
    - `Jump to oldest missing day`: sets the as-of date to the oldest unreconciled day.
    - `Run auto-catchup (verified zero variance)`: opens the auto-catchup dialog to bulk-close
      missing days with zero variance (ADMIN only, requires physical verification note).
    - `View all missing days`: expandable list with per-day `Open day` buttons.
  - Unreconciled days (Ledger mode, amber card): informational only — tells the user to switch to
    Operational mode to close the missing day records before running a ledger reconciliation.
  - Day already reconciled (sky/blue card): shown when the as-of date already has one or more
    reconciliation records. Displays the record count, latest reconciler, and latest variance.
    - To resave the day: check `Allow override (resave reconciled day)`, enter a reason of at least
      10 characters, then proceed with the form. Overriding creates an additional record for the
      same day rather than replacing the existing one.

  Step 2 — Expected balance (left column):
  - Operational mode shows three summary items: Cash in (day), Cash out (day), and Expected day net
    (in minus out). The expected day net is the target value the cashier's count should match.
  - Ledger mode shows only the GL cash balance as-of the selected date.
  - Both modes always show the "Expected value for reconciliation" item, which is the figure the
    counted amount will be compared against.
  - Source breakdown (Operational mode only): a per-source grid showing cash in, cash out, and net
    for each source type (e.g., Walk-in OTC, Online orders, Account collections, Manual). Net
    values use signed colour coding: red for negative (net cash out), green for positive (net cash
    in), no colour for zero.
  - Till calculator (Operational mode only, optional): enter the opening till float and counted
    closing till balance. The calculator shows the counted day net (closing minus opening) and the
    till-vs-ledger day variance. This is a local helper only — it does not pre-fill the count form.
    Use it when the cashier counts the full till rather than tracking intraday net movement.

  Step 3 — Record cash count (right column):
  - Counted amount field: enter the physical count figure. For Operational mode this is the counted
    net movement; for Ledger mode this is the total physical cash in the drawer.
  - Variance preview: appears as soon as a counted amount is entered. Shows the difference between
    expected and actual, with direction label (shortage / overage / balanced) and signed colour
    (red for shortage, green for overage).
  - Variance reason (appears only when variance is non-zero): required dropdown — Count error,
    Unrecorded payout, Timing difference, Suspected shrinkage, or Other.
  - Variance explanation (appears only when variance is non-zero): required free-text field with
    500-character limit and live character counter.
  - Notes (appears when variance is zero): optional free-text field for any remarks.
  - Pre-submission checklist: three checkboxes that must all be ticked before the Save button
    becomes effective:
    1. Physical cash count completed.
    2. Any discrepancy reviewed and validated.
    3. Notes/reason captured where required.
  - Save cash reconciliation button: disabled until the counted amount field is non-empty.
    On success: clears the form, shows a success toast, refreshes the history.
    On failure: shows an error toast with the server reason.
  - Deposit reminder: a hint line below the save button shows why the Deposit button in the page
    header is currently blocked (wrong mode, or day not yet reconciled).

  Page header actions:
  - Export CSV: exports summary, source breakdown, and reconciliation records for the selected day
    into a CSV file named `cash-reconciliation-{date}-{mode}.csv`.
  - Print Report: opens a formatted HTML report in a new browser tab and triggers the print dialog.
    The report includes the summary table, source breakdown, and reconciliation records for the
    selected day, with signature lines for preparer and reviewer at the bottom.
  - Deposit Cash to Bank: opens a dialog to record a cash-to-bank transfer. Only available in
    Operational mode after the selected day is reconciled.
    - Select the destination bank account from the configured bank list.
    - Enter the deposit amount (must be positive).
    - Optional notes.
    - On save: posts a journal entry debiting Bank and crediting Cash (account 1000), and shows a
      success toast.

  Auto-catchup dialog (ADMIN only):
  - Opened from the unreconciled-days amber banner.
  - Lists all unreconciled days with individual checkboxes (all pre-selected).
  - `Select all` and `Deselect all` toggle buttons with a live selected-count display.
  - Verification checkbox: "I verified each selected day physically and confirmed zero variance."
    Must be ticked before the run button activates.
  - Verification note: required free-text, minimum 10 characters, with character counter and
    below-minimum warning.
  - Run button: labelled with the selected day count (e.g., "Run auto-catchup (3 days)"). Disabled
    until all conditions are met. Creates zero-variance records (expected = actual, no journal
    entry) for each selected day, skipping any already reconciled.

  Reconciliation history (bottom of page):
  - Card header contains:
    - Title "Reconciliation history".
    - Stats badges: total record count, non-zero variance count (amber badge when > 0), latest
      variance amount (red badge for shortage, green for overage, hidden when zero).
    - Active filter scope label (e.g., "ALL / ALL VARIANCES").
    - Quick preset buttons: `Today non-zero`, `This month`, `All non-zero` — each applies a
      preset combination of range and variance filter and resets to page 1.
    - `Filters` toggle button: expands / collapses the filter bar. Button becomes filled when
      the bar is open.
    - `Export CSV` button: downloads the current page of records as a CSV. The success toast
      states how many rows were exported out of the total (e.g., "10 of 47 rows — current page").
  - Collapsible filter bar (inside the card header, visible when Filters is open):
    - Range: Today, This month, All.
    - From / To: custom date range (works alongside the range preset).
    - Variance filter: All or Non-zero only.
    - Rows per page: 10, 25, 50.
    - Saved filters: a select dropdown of filters saved to localStorage per cash account.
      - To save the current filter state: click `Save current`, type a name in the inline input
        (press Enter or click `Save`, press Escape to cancel), confirm.
      - To apply a saved filter: choose it from the dropdown.
      - To delete a saved filter: select it then click `Delete filter`.
    - `Clear filters` button: resets range to All, clears from/to, sets variance to All.
  - History table:
    - Columns: Date / Account, Mode, Expected, Actual, Variance, Journal, By, (expand button).
    - Date / Account cell: shows date and time (to disambiguate same-day multi-records), account
      code and name on a second line, and the notes field on a third line in italic if present.
    - Mode badge: LEDGER (sky) or OPERATIONAL (emerald).
    - Variance column: signed colour (red for shortage with "(short)" label, green for overage
      with "(over)" label, no colour for zero).
    - Journal column: when a journal entry was posted for the variance, shows the first 8
      characters of the journal entry ID as a clickable link to
      `/admin/accounting/journal?entryId=...`. Shows dash when no journal entry exists.
    - By column: the name or email of the user who created the record, or "System".
    - `Details` / `Hide` expand button: fetches and shows the operational source breakdown for
      that day inline below the row. Cached after the first fetch.
  - Pagination: shows page X of Y and total count, with Prev and Next buttons.

- API-level test coverage (vitest — run with `pnpm test:vitest`):
  The route.vitest.spec.ts for cash-reconciliations covers 14 tests:
  - Auth guards: rejects null session and insufficient role (non-ADMIN/ACCOUNTANT) with 401/403.
  - CSRF protection: rejects requests with invalid origin.
  - Input validation: required fields (actualAmount, countedAt, mode), invalid enum values.
  - Cash account resolution: returns 404 when no cash account exists and none can be resolved.
  - Duplicate day handling: returns 409 with DAY_ALREADY_RECONCILED code when saving a day that
    already has a reconciliation record without the allowReopenOverride flag; accepts override with
    a reason of at least 10 characters.
  - Variance validation: rejects saves where variance is non-zero but varianceReason or notes are
    missing.
  - Closed period check: returns 400 when the target date falls in a closed accounting period.
  - Journal entry creation: creates a journal entry only when variance is non-zero; skips when zero.
  All 14 tests pass. Run:
  `pnpm test:vitest -- src/app/api/admin/accounting/cash-reconciliations/route.vitest.spec.ts`

Opening Balance — How, Where, When, and What Not To Do
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

What is the opening balance?
  The opening balance anchor is a one-time verified physical count that sets the authoritative
  starting point for the cash account in the GL. After it is set:
  - The GL balance is guaranteed to match what was physically in the drawer on the anchor date.
  - All future Operational (scope=all) expected amounts are derived as GL delta from this point
    (ledger_balance_today minus ledger_balance_yesterday), making them identical in source to the
    Ledger mode.
  - The Operational vs Ledger divergence in cash reconciliation is structurally eliminated going
    forward (for days with complete GL posting on the correct date).

Where to set it:
  Page: /admin/accounting/cash-reconciliations
  Button: `Set Opening Balance` — top-right of the page header, alongside Export CSV, Print
  Report, and Deposit Cash to Bank.

How to set it (step by step):
  1. Count the physical cash in the drawer. Write down the exact figure.
  2. Open `/admin/accounting/cash-reconciliations`.
  3. Click `Set Opening Balance` in the page header.
  4. The dialog shows: current GL balance (what the system thinks the drawer contains) and the
     selected cash account code and name.
  5. Enter your verified physical count in the `Verified physical count` field.
  6. The dialog immediately previews the required GL adjustment:
     - Green box: "GL matches your count — no adjustment needed." (counts match exactly)
     - Amber box: "GL adjustment required: +X.XX" or "-X.XX" (GL needs to be corrected)
  7. Select the `Anchor date` (defaults to today). Use the date the physical count was performed.
  8. Leave `Post a GL adjustment journal entry` checked (recommended) unless you are certain the
     GL is already correct.
  9. Add optional notes (e.g., "End-of-year physical count by management, witnessed by owner").
  10. Click `Set Opening Balance`.
      - If GL adjustment is non-zero: a journal entry is posted to debit or credit the cash
        account (1000) against Opening Balance Equity (3900), bringing the GL to exactly match
        your count.
      - A CashReconciliation record is created marked OPENING BAL with variance = 0.
      - A toast shows the adjustment amount (or confirms no adjustment was needed).

When to set it:
  - At the very start of using this system, before any reconciliations are saved.
  - After any period where the GL history is unreliable (e.g., following a data migration, a
    system reset, or discovering the GL was not being updated).
  - At the start of a new fiscal year when you want a clean verified starting point.
  - Do NOT set it in the middle of an active period without also running a physical count — an
    anchor set to an incorrect figure will corrupt future expected amounts.

Opening balance constraints:
  - Only ONE opening balance anchor is allowed per cash account. If you need to reset it, contact
    a developer to delete the existing OPENING BAL record from the database.
  - The anchor date must fall in an open fiscal period if a GL adjustment is needed.
  - The dialog does not accept negative counts.
  - If `Post GL adjustment` is unchecked, the anchor is recorded but the GL is left as-is (use
    this only if you have already manually corrected the GL via a journal entry and simply want the
    reconciliation marker).

What to do if the opening balance was set incorrectly:
  - Contact an administrator to have the OPENING BAL reconciliation record deleted from the
    database, and the associated journal entry (if any) reversed.
  - Then set a new opening balance with the correct count.

Preventing and Eliminating Variance
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Understanding the two types of variance:

  Type 1 — Cash reconciliation variance (physical vs expected):
    actual_amount - expected_amount
    This is the most important variance. It measures whether the physical cash in the drawer
    matches what the system expects. A non-zero value means money is unaccounted for. This
    variance should NEVER be ignored or zeroed out without a documented reason.

  Type 2 — Operational vs Ledger mode divergence (mode mismatch):
    expected_operational - expected_ledger_delta
    This is the variance between how two different calculation modes compute the expected amount
    for a given day. As of the current implementation, both Operational (scope=all) and Ledger
    modes derive from the same GL data, so this should be structurally zero for days with complete
    and timely GL posting.

Rules to prevent Type 1 variance (cash shortages/overages):

  DO:
  - Reconcile every trading day without gaps. Use auto-catchup for days where GL movements were
    zero and no cash changed hands.
  - Count cash at the end of every shift, before banking.
  - Have a second person verify counts above a threshold (e.g., GH₵500 difference).
  - Record the exact count — do not round to a "close enough" figure.
  - Post all journal entries on the date the transaction actually occurred (same-day posting).
  - When a variance is found, record the correct variance reason and a detailed note explaining
    what caused it. Do not select `COUNT_ERROR` reflexively — only use it if the count was
    genuinely incorrect.
  - Run a physical cash count at month-end and confirm it matches the closing GL balance.

  DO NOT:
  - Do not reconcile without physically counting — guessing the count creates false zero-variance
    records that hide real problems.
  - Do not back-date reconciliations to cover gaps without physically verifying those days.
  - Do not post adjustment journal entries in bulk to zero out a growing variance without
    investigating the root cause.
  - Do not use override (resave) to change a reconciliation without a documented reason.
  - Do not mark a non-zero variance as a "count error" when the actual cause is unknown.

Rules to prevent Type 2 variance (Operational vs Ledger mode divergence):

  DO:
  - Use Operational mode (scope=all) as the default. It now derives expected amounts from the GL
    balance delta, ensuring it is always consistent with the Ledger.
  - Set an opening balance anchor before the first reconciliation to establish a verified GL
    starting point.
  - Post all journal entries on the correct transaction date. Late-posted or backdated entries
    will cause a one-day spike in the delta (the entry shows up in the wrong day's GL balance).
  - Use auto-catchup for days with zero GL movement to keep the reconciliation chain unbroken.
    A gap in the chain allows cumulative delta drift.
  - At the end of each month, confirm the sum of all daily Operational expected amounts for the
    month equals the Ledger balance change over the same month. If they differ, a journal entry
    was posted on the wrong date.

  DO NOT:
  - Do not mix Operational and Ledger mode records for the same account without a documented
    reason. Inconsistent mode usage makes the reconcile page variance table harder to interpret.
  - Do not skip the opening balance anchor and expect the two modes to align — without an anchor,
    there is no verified starting point from which to derive accurate deltas.
  - Do not post GL entries without a corresponding business event (e.g., do not manually debit
    Cash to inflate the balance). This creates a phantom expected amount that no physical count
    can match.

How to diagnose and fix a growing variance:
  1. Open `/admin/accounting/cash-reconciliations`. Check `Unreconciled days` — if gaps exist,
     the cumulative drift grows daily.
  2. Check `Ledger diagnostics` — if the GL balance is negative, the GL was never anchored
     correctly. Use `Set Opening Balance` to correct it.
  3. Open `/admin/accounting/reconcile`. Look at the manual entries tab — if entries exist that
     do not correspond to real cash events (sales, payroll, purchases), they are corrupting the
     expected amount.
  4. Filter the history table to Non-zero only. Look for patterns: same-day repeating variances
     suggest a recurring unrecorded event (e.g., petty cash payments not entered); random
     variances suggest counting errors or undocumented cash out.
  5. If the variance is large and unexplained, escalate to management before posting any
     adjustments. Post the correction as a SUSPECTED_SHRINKAGE variance with a full explanation.

      level 1 at 4 overdue hours, level 2 at 24 overdue hours.
      Escalation events are audit-logged as `HEALTH_INCIDENT_ESCALATION_SENT` or `HEALTH_INCIDENT_ESCALATION_SKIPPED`.
      Scheduled escalation checks:
      configure cron endpoint `/api/admin/health/escalations/cron` (secret via `HEALTH_INCIDENT_ESCALATION_CRON_SECRET` or `CRON_SECRET`).
      Both actions use confirmation dialogs and write audit logs (`HEALTH_ALERT_DRY_RUN` / `HEALTH_ALERT_SENT`).
  23) Use `Incident timeline` to add investigation notes as work progresses.
      `Add note` requires at least 8 characters and writes `HEALTH_INCIDENT_NOTE_ADDED` to audit log.
      Use this for handoff context between admins (what was checked, what changed, next step).
      Incident mode control:
      - `Detector-backed`: use when active issue signals exist.
      - `Operational follow-up`: use manual documentation when no active issue signals exist.
      If no active signals are present, select `Operational follow-up` (detector-backed mode is blocked).
      Detector-backed warning behavior: the warning now appears only when a user starts typing a timeline note while still in `Detector-backed` mode and there are no active issue signals.
      In this blocked detector-backed state, incident note input is disabled and the 8-character note warning is suppressed.
      Optional follow-up due date can be set for manual follow-up incidents.
      Incident records are now stored per Health incident (not a single global list).
      The Health page shows the latest 5 notes for the current/recent incident to keep the page clean.
      Full historical notes remain available in audit logs.
      Incident timeline tutorial:
      1. After diagnostics, add a first note with the finding.
      2. After each repair action (auto-heal/manual), add outcome note.
      3. Before handoff, add next-step note so another admin can continue.
      4. At closure, add final resolution note (what fixed it and verification result).
      Suggested note format: `Finding -> Action -> Outcome -> Next step`.
      If no active issue-linked incident exists, adding a note now auto-creates a manual incident so notes are never lost.
  24) Use `Runbook checklist` in Health Operations to track execution status:
      diagnostics run, owner/due assignment, investigation note added, healthy-state verification, and report handoff.
      When no issue signals are active, the runbook area shows `No active incident` instead of completed/pending items.
      Runbook checklist tutorial:
      1. Run diagnostics.
      2. Assign owner + due date.
      3. Add at least one incident timeline note.
      4. Re-run diagnostics after repair and verify healthy state.
      5. Export report for handoff/records.
      Keep Health workflow status as `Open` or `In progress` while checklist items remain pending.
      Incident lifecycle behavior:
      - Diagnostics with active issue signals opens/updates an incident.
      - Healthy diagnostics resolves the active incident.
      - New issue fingerprint opens a new incident timeline.
      `Runbook by signal` card now tracks category-level status:
      Posting/Ledger, Stock integrity, Payments/Balances, and POD compliance.
      KPI strip at top now shows:
      MTTA, MTTR, reopen rate, and overdue rate (rolling 30 days) for operational quality tracking.
      KPI trend sparklines:
      weekly MTTA and MTTR mini charts help detect trend direction, not just current average.
  25) Export `health report (CSV)` or `summary (PDF)` when sharing health review outcomes.
      `Export handoff bundle` adds a one-click text package containing:
      snapshot summary, workflow context, signal breakdown, incident context, latest timeline notes, and 7-run trend.
      Handoff filter controls:
      choose `Scope` (`Full snapshot` or `Incident only`) and `Timeline notes` limit (5/10/20),
      then click `Download configured handoff bundle`.
      Exports now include workflow ownership fields (status, due date, overdue flag, needs-assignment flag).
      PDF export uses the same stable generator approach as Audit exports for local/dev reliability.
      Each export now writes an audit log row (`HEALTH_EXPORT_CSV` / `HEALTH_EXPORT_PDF` / `HEALTH_EXPORT_HANDOFF_BUNDLE`)
      with initiator, file details, size, and workflow scope snapshot.
      Recommended operating sequence:
      (a) Check severity + issue count,
      (b) choose queue preset,
      (c) run diagnostics,
      (d) acknowledge owner/note,
      (e) run auto-heal if appropriate,
      (f) rerun diagnostics,
      (g) export report for handoff.
  26) Open `/admin/health/incidents` for incident-focused history and handoff:
      - Manual incidents are hidden by default; turn on `Include manual incidents` when needed.
      - Default status view is `All statuses` so recent incidents are visible immediately.
      - Filter by status (`Open`, `In progress`, `Resolved`, `Closed`) and search summary/fingerprint/note text.
      - Use this page for historical review; use `/admin/health` for live response actions.
      - Incident list is mobile-card friendly and desktop-table friendly.
      - Desktop table now keeps fixed column spacing so status/opened/count/summary/fingerprint/resolved/latest-note remain aligned.
      - Each row shows status, opened/resolved timestamps, issue count, summary, fingerprint, and latest note.
      Suggested use:
      1. During active issue: keep working from `/admin/health` and add timeline notes.
      2. During handoff/reporting: open `/admin/health/incidents` to review recent incidents quickly.
      3. During audit prep: cross-check incident rows with `/admin/audit` entries.
      4. For parallel incidents, click `Manage` on each incident row/card and update that incident directly:
         - Set `Status` (`Open`, `In progress`, `Resolved`, `Closed`).
         - Assign an `Owner` (or keep `Unassigned`).
         - Set/adjust `Follow-up due date`.
         - Add optional `Workflow note` and confirm update.
      5. Add timeline updates from the same incident detail page (`Add note`), minimum 8 characters.
      6. This per-incident workflow is independent of the global checklist on `/admin/health`.
         Use `/admin/health` for current system snapshot response, and `/admin/health/incidents/[id]` for incident-specific tracking/handoff.
      7. Workflow update confirmation now has two actions:
         - `Confirm update`: save and stay on the incident page.
         - `Confirm update & back`: save and return to incident list.
         Success/failure is shown with toast notification and inline status text.
      8. Incident status lifecycle (industry standard):
         - `Resolved`: fix verified but can still be reopened if needed.
         - `Closed`: final administrative closure.
         - Reopening `Resolved` -> `Open/In progress` requires a reason (minimum 8 characters).
         - `Closed` incidents cannot be edited via normal workflow controls.

      10. Incident tools (detail page):
         - `Mark false positive`: closes incident with mandatory reason and logs `HEALTH_INCIDENT_FALSE_POSITIVE`.

         - `Merge source incident`: merges another incident into current and closes source, logged as `HEALTH_INCIDENT_MERGED`.
      9. Desktop incident table readability:
         - Columns are left-aligned with balanced spacing and clearer date/age formatting.
         - Long `Summary`, `Fingerprint`, and `Latest Note` values wrap cleanly.
         - Horizontal scroll remains available on narrower laptop widths.
- Auto-heal control: `Run auto-heal now` is enabled only when `ACCOUNTING_AUTO_HEAL_MISSING_POSTINGS=1` is set in `.env`/`.env.local`.

## B2B Procurement Workflow
- Page: /admin/b2b/procurement
- Access: ADMIN, STAFF, and ACCOUNTANT can view the queue. ADMIN and STAFF perform assignment, status, tender, draft-order, and bulk actions; admin-only audit links are visible to ADMIN.
- Purpose: Work clinic procurement requests from submission through review, quote, approval, tender preparation, draft order conversion, rejection, close, and reopen.

Top-to-bottom walkthrough
1) Header actions
   - `Analytics dashboard` opens /admin/b2b/procurement/analytics.
   - `Tender builder` opens /admin/b2b/tenders.
   - `View audit log` opens the page-scoped audit view for B2B procurement request events.
   - `Export CSV` downloads the current filtered queue and records an audit event with filters and row count.
2) Queue summary
   - `Visible requests` shows the number of rows currently returned for the page.
   - `Open on page` counts non-terminal requests visible on the page.
   - `Unassigned open` highlights rows needing manager assignment.
   - `SLA risk` counts visible open rows whose age has crossed the page warning thresholds.
3) Filters
   - `Queue` supports Open, Closed, Archived, and All.
   - `Type` filters Quote, PO Upload, and Recurring Reorder requests.
   - `Manager` filters all managers, a specific manager, or Unassigned.
   - `From` and `To` filter by request creation date. Invalid ranges are blocked client-side.
   - `Search` filters clinic, contact, customer, email, phone, or request ID and accepts URL-driven `search` links from analytics.
   - `Clear filters` resets search, type, manager, and date filters.
   - `Auto-archive closed requests after` changes the age threshold for moving closed/rejected rows into the archived queue.
4) Bulk actions
   - Select one or more visible rows to open the bulk action strip.
   - `Apply Assign` assigns the selected manager and auto-promotes submitted requests to In Review.
   - `Apply Status` applies a selected status to all selected rows.
   - Requests cannot bulk move to Quoted or Approved until each selected row already has a manager. Use `Apply Assign` first.
   - Bulk actions write item-level audit records plus a summary audit record with success and failure counts.
5) Request rows
   - Each row shows clinic, status, request type, age/SLA chip, contact links, customer identity, submitted date, assigned manager, and row-scoped audit link for admins.
   - Closed, rejected, and archived rows are collapsed by default. Expand to inspect details or reopen.
   - `Assign` requires a manager and logs before/after manager metadata.
   - `Update Status` requires an explicit new status; no-op updates are blocked.
   - `Reopen` is available for terminal rows and requires a workflow note.
   - `Create Tender` opens Tender Builder with the procurement request context.
   - `Draft Order` prepares order lines from the request/template and opens /admin/orders/new. Successes and blocked attempts are audited.
   - `Requested items` and `Open uploaded PO document` are read-only reference fields for quoting and order conversion.

## Tender Builder
- Page: /admin/b2b/tenders
- Access: ADMIN and STAFF.
- Purpose:
  - Build branded B2B tender PDFs from pasted lists, uploaded files, or procurement requests.
  - Review product matching, stock risk, margin risk, and no-bid/substitute decisions before saving.
  - Submit, approve, send, compare versions, track expiry, export tender packages, and convert WON tenders into orders.
- Related pages:
  - /admin/b2b/procurement
  - /admin/orders/new
  - /admin/audit
- Core rule:
  - Tender creation and editing happen in the Build Tender tab.
  - Email sending happens only after a tender is submitted.
  - Final statuses are locked.

Top-to-bottom walkthrough

1) Page header
   - Title: `Tender Builder`.
   - `New Tender` clears the build form and returns to a fresh draft workspace.
   - Admin-only `View Audit Log` opens `/admin/audit?sourcePage=admin%2Fb2b%2Ftenders`.
   - `Back to Procurement` returns to the procurement request workflow.

2) Expiry banner
   - Appears only when the reminder feed contains tenders expiring soon.
   - Each tender number in the banner is clickable.
   - Clicking a tender number opens the Tenders tab and applies a search for that tender number.

3) Workflow summary strip
   - `Drafting`: count of draft tenders currently visible in the active server-backed result set.
   - `Ready to send`: count of visible tenders eligible for email sending.
   - `Expiring soon`: count from the reminder feed.
   - `Visible results`: current page count and total matching tender count.
   - Use this strip as a quick operational health check before starting work.

4) Tabs
   - `Build Tender`: create or revise tender content.
   - `Send & Versions`: approve, email, preview PDF, and compare versions.
   - `Tenders`: search, filter, update status, export, remind, convert, and open audit trails.
   - The tab controls are keyboard and screen-reader friendly: they use tablist/tab/tabpanel semantics.

Build Tender tab

5) Source and buyer details
   - Source options:
     - `Paste / Upload`
     - `From Procurement Request`
   - Buyer fields:
     - Buyer / Facility
     - Buyer contact
     - Buyer email
     - Tender reference
   - If the tender reference matches a procurement request, a `View request` link appears.

6) Procurement request source
   - Appears when Source is `From Procurement Request`.
   - Active source statuses are:
     - SUBMITTED
     - IN_REVIEW
     - QUOTED
   - Closed/rejected requests are not shown while active requests exist.
   - `Load Request` fills buyer details, tender reference, and item text from the selected procurement request.

7) Commercial setup
   - Fields:
     - Lot title
     - Currency
     - Validity days
     - Upload TXT / CSV / PDF / DOCX / Image
     - VAT %
     - Discount Amount
     - Freight Amount
     - Handling Amount
     - Margin Lock % (min)
     - Global Lead Time (days)
     - Payment Terms
   - Currency defaults to GHS when no value is provided.
   - Margin Lock prevents saving lines below the configured minimum margin, except NO_BID lines.

8) Template packs
   - Use templates to reuse commercial terms.
   - Apply a saved template from the template dropdown.
   - Save a template by entering:
     - template name
     - source type
     - current validity, lead time, payment terms, and notes
   - Delete template uses a confirmation dialog.

   - Paste one item per line.
   - Supported examples:
     - `Paracetamol 500mg tabs, box, 100`
     - `Paracetamol 500mg tabs: 100`

   - OCR/image extraction requires the server OCR configuration to be enabled.

10) Analyze Items
   - Click `Analyze Items` after buyer and item details are ready.
   - The preview matches items against the product catalog and calculates matched count, unmatched count, subtotal, VAT, freight, handling, discount, and total.
   - The preview table shows requested item, matched product, quantity, stock, cost, margin, unit price, total, and match confidence.

11) Line review
   - Lines are flagged for review when they are low confidence, unmatched, suspicious, substitute, or out of stock.
   - For unmatched/low-confidence items:
     - choose a catalog product from suggestions, or
     - mark as `No Bid (Discontinued)`, or
     - mark as `Substitute Offered`.
   - For out-of-stock lines:
     - enter lead time days;
     - include a split supply note that mentions available-now quantity and balance quantity;
     - use `Clear OOS` only after the required note is complete.
   - Before saving, check `I reviewed flagged lines and confirm tender values are correct.`

12) Save Tender Draft
   - Saving creates or updates a DRAFT tender.
   - A new tender version is created for each save.
   - Audit action:
     - `B2B_TENDER_SAVED` for new tender
     - `B2B_TENDER_UPDATED` for draft update
   - Audit metadata includes source page, operation, version number, tender number, buyer, status, currency, total, line count, and actor details.
   - After save, the last-saved banner shows tender number, status, total, updated date, PDF action, edit action, and send guidance.

13) Editing and revising
   - Editing a DRAFT loads it for direct update.
   - Loading a non-draft tender into Build acts as a template for a new draft, not a direct edit of the finalized tender.
   - Loading a draft revalidates stock and pricing against the current catalog.

Send & Versions tab

14) Tender selection
   - The dropdown lists only send-eligible tenders:
     - SUBMITTED
     - SENT
   - DRAFT tenders must be submitted first.
   - WON, LOST, EXPIRED, and CANCELLED tenders cannot be emailed from this workflow.
   - If a selected tender has buyer email, the `To` field is prefilled when empty.

   - The approval banner explains whether the selected tender can be sent.

     - B2B_TENDER_REQUIRE_APPROVAL=1 requires latest-version approval.
     - B2B_TENDER_REQUIRE_APPROVAL=0 disables the approval gate.

   - Available only after selecting a send-eligible tender.

   - Requires selected send-eligible tender, recipient email, and approval when approval gate is enabled.

   - If CC fails but primary recipient succeeds:
     - the API returns a CC warning;
     - tender remains SENT;
     - TO recipient is recorded as SENT;
     - CC recipient is recorded as FAILED with lastError;

   - If primary recipient fails:
     - tender status is not changed;
     - audit action `B2B_TENDER_SEND_FAILED` is written with FAILED outcome and delivery error metadata.
   - Successful send audit actions:
     - `B2B_TENDER_SENT`
     - `B2B_TENDER_RESENT`
   - Send audit metadata includes source page, operation, before/after status, tender number, buyer, selected version, latest version, recipient statuses, delivery channel, timestamp, outcome, and actor details.

  - Orders updated as delivered outside dispatch context (e.g., order page pickup) should not trigger false POD missing follow-up.

### Failed/Rescheduled Custody Handover
- Problem solved:
  - Failed/rescheduled trips can still be physically with the dispatcher.
- Implemented behavior:
  1) Dispatcher marks `FAILED_ATTEMPT` or `RESCHEDULED`.
  2) System flags `return pending handover`.
  3) Order remains visible to that dispatcher until handover is confirmed.
  4) Admin uses `Confirm return received` in Dispatch actions.
  5) System clears assignment after confirmation; rider no longer appears as current assignment on that order.
- Reassignment policy:
  - Reassign is blocked while return handover is pending.
  - Unassign is also blocked while return handover is pending.
  - Confirm return first, then reassign.

### Collection Claims (Dispatcher -> Admin Confirm)
- Recommended control flow (implemented):
  1) Dispatcher marks order delivered.
  2) Dispatcher records collection claim (amount/method/reference/note) when applicable.
  3) Admin confirms claim from Dispatch board (`Confirm + Post Payment`).
  4) Only on admin confirmation does official payment ledger/journal posting occur.
- Why:
  - Prevents unverified field collections from posting directly to finance.
  - Maintains separation between operational claim and accounting confirmation.

### Unassign / Reassign
- `Actions` menu includes unassign with modal confirmation and reason.
- Use when rider unavailable, wrong assignment, or reroute is needed.
- Reassign by running assign flow again.

### Delivery Scope Model (Important)

  - Dispatcher delivers what was assigned on manifest.

Accounting and control rules
- Manual category rules:
  - Manual expenses must use active EXPENSE accounts.
  - System-driven accounts such as COGS, Payroll Expense, and Cash Over/Short are blocked from manual expense posting.
- Posting rules:
  - Paid-now expense: debit selected expense account and credit the selected payment clearing account.
  - Accrued expense: debit selected expense account and credit `2300 Accrued Expenses`.
  - Settlement: debit `2300 Accrued Expenses` and credit the selected payment account.
  - Partial settlements are supported and reduce outstanding balance until the expense is fully paid.
  - Reversals post as negative linked expenses against the original expense.
- Mutation lock rules:
  - Direct edit/delete is blocked for deleted expenses, reversal rows, payroll-generated expenses, expenses with settlements, expenses with reversals, and expenses outside the 48-hour edit window.
  - Locked expenses should be corrected through settlement, reversal, or accounting adjustment rather than silent mutation.
  - The UI must show the lock reason instead of hiding the problem.
- Required audit behavior:
  - Create, update, delete, restore, settlement, reversal, and CSV export actions must be audit logged.
  - Blocked update, delete, and settlement attempts must also be audit logged with failed outcome metadata.
  - Useful metadata includes sourcePage, section/operation, expense ID, category, amount, vendor, payment mode, settlement sequence, total expense amount, paid/outstanding values, journal entry IDs, lock code, lock reason, settlement count, reversal count, and before/after values where applicable.
